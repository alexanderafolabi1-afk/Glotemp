What is Food?

Food measures the vibrancy and quality of a city's culinary scene. It combines restaurant density, Michelin star density, culinary innovation and awards, food festival calendars, and cuisine diversity indices to show where exceptional dining and food culture thrive.

Data Sources

  • OpenStreetMap: Restaurant listings and dining venue data
  • Michelin Guides: Award-winning and starred restaurants
  • Culinary Data: Cuisine diversity and food culture metrics
