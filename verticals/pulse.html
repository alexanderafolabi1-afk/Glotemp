What is Pulse?

Pulse measures the emotional temperature of a city right now. It combines human check-ins, sentiment analysis from global news streams (GDELT), and local event signals to show whether a city feels energized, contemplative, or somewhere in between.

Data Sources

  • User Check-ins: Direct mood reports from travelers
  • GDELT Sentiment: Global news and social media sentiment analysis
  • Event Signals: Concert, festival, and cultural event calendars
