What is Entertainment?

Entertainment measures the cultural vibrancy and nightlife energy of a city. It combines event calendars (concerts, theater, festivals), venue counts (museums, theaters, nightclubs), and real-time energy assessments to show where the cultural and social scene is thriving.

Data Sources

  • Ticketmaster API: Live events and entertainment venue data
  • Cultural Calendars: Theater, museum, and gallery information
  • Nightlife Data: Nightclub density and entertainment venue metrics
