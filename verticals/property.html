What is Property?

Property tracks real estate opportunity and housing market dynamics across cities. It combines rental price indices, property appreciation trends, housing availability metrics, and market velocity to identify cities with strong investment potential or affordable living.

Data Sources

  • OpenStreetMap: Rental listings and housing density data
  • Real Estate Markets: Property appreciation and price trends
  • Housing Indicators: Construction starts and housing availability
