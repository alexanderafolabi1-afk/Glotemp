What is Transport?

Transport measures the mobility infrastructure and transportation efficiency of a city. It combines public transit system quality and coverage, traffic congestion levels, bike-sharing density, pedestrian infrastructure, and commute time efficiency to identify cities where getting around is convenient and sustainable.

Data Sources

  • Transitland API: Public transit stops, routes, and schedules
  • OpenStreetMap: Bike infrastructure and pedestrian routes
  • Traffic Data: Congestion indices and commute time metrics
