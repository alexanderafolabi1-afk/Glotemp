What is Health?

Health measures the overall well-being and healthcare infrastructure of a city. It combines air quality indices, hospital ratings and accessibility, life expectancy, disease prevalence, mental health and stress levels, and wellness indices to show where people thrive physically and mentally.

Data Sources

  • WAQI (World Air Quality Index): Real-time air quality measurements
  • Healthcare Data: Hospital availability and quality ratings
  • Wellness Indicators: Overall wellness and fitness metrics
