What is Sport?

Sport measures a city's athletic culture and sports infrastructure. It tracks sports venues and facilities, active participation rates in recreational sports, major sporting events and competitions hosted annually, and the vitality of the sports community.

Data Sources

  • TheSportsDB: Sports venues, events, and team data
  • Sports Infrastructure: Stadium and facility counts and capacity
  • Participation Data: Active sports engagement and recreational participation
