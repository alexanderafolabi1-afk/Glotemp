What is Fashion?

Fashion measures a city's influence and prominence in the global style ecosystem. It combines the presence of major designer brands, frequency of fashion events and runway shows, global fashion news mentions, and trendiness indices to identify the centers of style and luxury retail.

Data Sources

  • Fashion Events: Fashion week schedules and major events
  • Luxury Retail: Designer brand store locations and density
  • Fashion News: GDELT mentions of fashion brands and style trends
