One roll, one lost city

Hidden gem

Roll the die. It lands on a city that was lost — either a real one that was buried, abandoned or forgotten and then dug back out of the ground, or one that was only ever a story. Every result says which of the two you got. No readings here, no live data: this is history and legend, kept apart.

 

The die is cold. Give it a throw.
