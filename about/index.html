About Glotemp

Every city breathes with its own rhythm. In the streets, in the cafés, in the transit hubs, there exists an invisible current of collective energy that shapes the character of urban spaces. This is the heartbeat of a city. And until now, it has remained invisible to those who long to understand it.

The moment

Imagine knowing the true pulse of a destination before you arrive. Not through algorithms that predict based on history, but through the living, breathing sentiment of thousands of people sharing their experience in real time. That moment, when expectation meets reality, when data becomes wisdom, is Glotemp.

For travellers

Travel is about discovery and connection. Glotemp changes the way you experience cities by revealing their true energy. Rather than following curated guides or outdated reviews, you tap into the collective consciousness of a city happening now. Should you visit that neighbourhood tonight? Is the city welcoming? What is the true vibe? These answers come not from algorithms, but from the authentic experiences of your fellow travellers.

For cities and communities

Urban planners, hospitality leaders and community organisers have always operated on incomplete data. Event organisers wonder if their gathering resonates. City authorities seek to understand neighbourhood sentiment. Local businesses want to know if they are truly serving their community. Glotemp closes this gap, turning anonymous check-ins into actionable intelligence that helps cities understand and serve their people better.

The philosophy

This is not surveillance. This is permission-based transparency. Every contribution is voluntary, a moment when someone chooses to share how a place makes them feel. Aggregated across thousands of voices, these moments reveal patterns that matter. The collective mood becomes a resource: for travellers seeking authenticity, for cities seeking understanding, for communities seeking connection.

Glotemp is the infrastructure that turns the feeling of cities into intelligence. Real time. Real people. Real value.
