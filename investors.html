Revenue Architecture

Four streams. All compounding.

Four complementary revenue streams that compound as the network grows. No single point of dependency.

01
City Sponsorships
Brands claim city sponsorships on monthly or annual contracts. Pricing scales with city pulse volume and market tier. Entry at $500/mo, premium cities $5,000+/mo. Native, contextual, and non-intrusive.
02
Pulse Data API
Licensed access to mood, scene, and momentum data for travel apps, tourism boards, insurance platforms, and urban planners. Tiered SaaS — $99 to $2,499/mo. The data becomes more valuable the more cities we cover.
03
Premium Contributor Tier
Power contributors unlock premium analytics, early city access, and enhanced Star multipliers via a $9.99/mo subscription, creating a predictable Monthly Recurring Revenue base from the most engaged users.
04
Trip Intelligence Reports
On-demand AI-generated city intelligence reports for travellers, event planners, and talent scouts. One-time purchase at $4.99–$19.99 per report. High margin, zero marginal cost after infrastructure.
