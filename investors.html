Revenue Architecture

Business Model

Four complementary revenue streams that compound as the network grows.

01
City Sponsorships
Brands claim city sponsorships on monthly or annual contracts. Pricing scales with city pulse volume and market tier. Entry at $500/mo, premium cities $5,000+/mo.
02
Pulse Data API
Licensed access to mood, scene, and momentum data for travel apps, tourism boards, insurance platforms, and urban planners. Tiered SaaS — $99 to $2,499/mo.
03
Premium Contributor Tier
Power contributors unlock premium analytics, early city access, and enhanced Star multipliers via a $9.99/mo subscription, creating a predictable MRR base.
04
Trip Intelligence Reports
On-demand AI-generated city intelligence reports for travellers, event planners, and talent scouts. One-time purchase at $4.99–$19.99 per report.

Network Effects

Contributor Economy

The contributors who build the data are the same people who own its value. This alignment is the moat.

🌐
Decentralised data sourcing
Every check-in from every Explorer strengthens the global signal. No single-point dependency — the network is the product.
⭐
Stars as equity proxy
Stars represent a contributor's proportional share of the community revenue pool. The more you contribute, the more you earn — creating compounding loyalty.
🏆
Rank as social capital
Luminary and Vanguard status are visible credentials that drive peer competition, retention, and organic advocacy — zero paid acquisition needed.
🗺️
Golden Path as narrative
The Explorer journey and city constellation visualisation turns contribution into a personal story — one that users share, building viral top-of-funnel loops.
