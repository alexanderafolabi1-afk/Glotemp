For local businesses

Offer something to a city

Glotemp is an instrument. It reads how a city feels right now — the mood, the hour, the radio, whether tonight is worth it — and keeps reading, city after city, day after day.

Partnering means putting your offer inside a reading that already exists. Hotels, restaurants, bars, clubs, cabs, cinemas, gyms, tours: give the person already looking at your city something to unlock and redeem. No app to install, no dashboard to learn. Every enquiry is read by a person before anything goes live.

Who this fits

Wherever your business sits, a vertical already reads it.

  • Hotels and tours Pulse, and what tonight looks like. Someone deciding where to stay or what to book is already checking both.
  • Restaurants Food — the vertical built for exactly this question: where to eat, and what it is like right now.
  • Bars, clubs, cinemas Entertainment — the reading that decides whether tonight is worth leaving the house for.
  • Cabs Transport. The read on getting around a city, at the moment someone is deciding whether to.
  • Gyms Sport. One of the same twelve verticals, read the same way in every city.
  • Tourism boards, and cities changing what they are known for Movers, and the city’s own reading. A city that is changing wants that change seen — this is where it shows first.
