About Glotemp

The world's pulse,
decoded in real time.

Glotemp is a living intelligence layer for the planet — mapping collective mood, energy, and cultural tempo across every city, every moment.

🌍

Our Mission

To give every person on earth a truthful, real-time signal about the emotional and energetic state of any city — so they can move smarter, connect deeper, and live more intentionally.

We believe collective human emotion is data. When structured and surfaced responsibly, it becomes a utility as fundamental as a weather forecast — informing travel decisions, business timing, creative work, and personal wellbeing at scale.

Core Technology

The Pulse Engine

A proprietary signal aggregation layer that fuses human check-ins, crowd intensity, scene context, and momentum patterns into a single composite city score.

⚡
Real-time aggregation
Check-ins are weighted by recency, intensity, and scene type to produce a live city score updated continuously.
🧠
Mood taxonomy
Five core emotional states — Excited, Calm, Stressed, Hopeful, Anxious — mapped against intensity and scene to generate nuanced city narratives.
📡
Momentum signals
Heat delta tracking captures rising and falling city energy, surfacing trend lines that predict short-term cultural momentum.
🗺️
Scene-layer intelligence
Club, University, Work, Tourism, Stadium, Street, and Home scene data are isolated and recombined to give granular context beyond a single headline score.

Sponsor Engine

Brands connect to the pulse. Sponsors appear contextually inside city cards, check-in moments, and the sponsor wall — never as interruptions.

🏙️
City sponsorships
A brand claims a city, unlocking a sponsored check-in banner and priority placement in city cards for that market.
🎯

🌟
